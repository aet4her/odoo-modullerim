Core Capabilities

Key Features Built for High-Velocity Teams

Seamlessly bridges Odoo Sales, Portal Payments, and Inventory Delivery Operations with your customers' favorite messaging channel.

One-Click Order WhatsApp

Send Quotations and confirmed Sales Orders directly from the sale.order header bar. Automatically compiles ordered items, quantities, subtotals, currency formatting, and the secure Customer Portal / Online Payment link.

Instant Shipping & Tracking Link

Keep customers informed the moment their parcel leaves the warehouse. Reads the Delivery Carrier (carrier_id), Tracking Reference, and Live Tracking URL from stock.picking and generates a ready-to-send dispatch alert.

Chatter Auto-Logging

Never lose track of customer touchpoints. Every time a salesperson or warehouse operator triggers a WhatsApp message, Odoo automatically posts a rich audit note in the document Chatter with recipient phone, order/delivery reference, and tracking details.

Zero API Cost & Smart Validation

Uses official WhatsApp universal deep links compatible with WhatsApp Web, Desktop, iOS, and Android — zero monthly API fees. Includes automatic international phone sanitization and friendly non-blocking toast notifications when a phone number is missing.

Simple Workflow

How It Works in 4 Easy Steps

Zero complex webhook setup or external gateways — up and running right after installation.

1
Install Module

Install WhatsApp Order & Shipping Pro from Apps. Buttons appear automatically in Sales and Inventory.

2
Send Quotation / Order

Open any Quotation or Sales Order and click the green Send by WhatsApp header button.

3
Dispatch Tracking

On any Delivery Order, click WhatsApp Tracking to share carrier & live tracking URLs in one click.

4
Automatic Chatter Log

WhatsApp opens pre-filled in a new tab while Odoo records a permanent activity log in Chatter.
